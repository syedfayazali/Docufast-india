Refund Policy

Our refund and cancellation terms for all DocuFast India services.

Effective Date: 1st January 2026 Last Updated: 13th October 2026 Jurisdiction: India

1. Overview

DocuFast India is committed to transparent and fair service. This Refund Policy explains the conditions under which refunds are issued.

DocuFast India collects service fees for our assistance, facilitation, and delivery services. These are separate from government fees. Government fees, once paid, are governed by the respective department's policies and are typically non-refundable.

2. Eligibility for Refund

A refund of DocuFast India's service fee may be considered in the following situations:

  • Service Not Commenced: If you cancel before we begin processing, a full refund (excluding transaction charges) will be issued
  • Error on Our Part: If an error solely by DocuFast India causes rejection or delay, we will re-process at no charge or issue a partial/full refund as appropriate
  • Service Unavailability: If we are unable to deliver the requested service due to circumstances within our control, a full refund will be issued
  • Duplicate Payment: If charged twice for the same service due to a payment error, the duplicate charge will be refunded promptly

3. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be issued in the following cases:

  • Government rejection due to the applicant's ineligibility, incorrect information, or incomplete documents
  • Delays caused by government processing timelines or system downtime
  • Change of mind after processing has commenced
  • Incorrect information submitted by the customer leading to rejection
  • Government fees already paid on your behalf
  • Courier or delivery charges once the shipment has been dispatched
  • Services fully delivered as requested

4. Cancellation Policy

  • Within 2 hours of placing a request: Full refund if processing has not yet started
  • After processing has commenced: No refund applicable
  • Doorstep orders: Once pickup is arranged or delivery is in transit, cancellation is not possible

To cancel, contact us immediately at support@docufastindia.com or +91 08542-355200 with your Order ID.

5. Refund Process

  • Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days from the date of approval
  • Credited to the original payment method used at the time of purchase
  • For cash payments, refunds are processed via bank transfer or UPI
  • You will be notified via email or SMS once the refund is processed
Please allow 3–5 additional business days for the refund to reflect in your account, depending on your bank or payment provider.

6. How to Request a Refund

Contact us with the following details:

  • Your full name and registered phone number
  • Your Order ID (format: DF-YYYY-XXX)
  • The service for which you are requesting a refund
  • Reason for your refund request
  • Proof of payment (screenshot or transaction reference)
  • Email: support@docufastindia.com
  • Phone: +91 08542-355200

7. Disputes

If you believe your refund request was unfairly declined, escalate by sending a written complaint to support@docufastindia.com. We will respond within 5 business days. Unresolved disputes are subject to the jurisdiction of courts in Mahabubnagar, Telangana, India.

8. Changes to This Policy

DocuFast India reserves the right to update this policy at any time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
