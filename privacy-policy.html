2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when using our services:

  • Identity Information: Full name, date of birth, gender
  • Contact Information: Phone number, email address, residential address
  • Document Information: Aadhaar, PAN, passport, driving licence, or voter ID details — only as required for the specific service
  • Payment Information: Transaction reference numbers (we do not store card or bank account details)
  • Usage Information: IP address, browser type, pages visited, collected automatically

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Process your document service applications and requests
  • Communicate order status and confirmations via SMS, email, or WhatsApp
  • Verify your identity as required for document processing
  • Coordinate doorstep pickup and delivery
  • Improve our services and customer experience
  • Comply with applicable laws or government requests
  • Detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized activity

4. Document Handling & Data Security

  • All submitted documents are handled only by authorized DocuFast India personnel
  • Physical documents are stored securely and returned or destroyed after processing
  • Digital data is stored on secured, encrypted systems
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party

Your Aadhaar, PAN, or passport data is used strictly for the service you requested and is never shared for commercial purposes.

We may share your data with trusted third parties only to fulfill your service:
